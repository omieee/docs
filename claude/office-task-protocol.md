# Office tasks: learn while you fix

Version 2, Oct 27 2026 (Om: keep it simple). Office time only, zero plan hours.

1. Do the task with an IBM-approved AI tool. Getting it done comes first.
2. Before the PR, ask the AI: "Teach me every Go and Kubernetes idea in this diff, one at a time, each with a tiny example I run." The diff is the syllabus.
3. Do not raise the PR until you can explain every changed line and one way it could break. Hard deadline: ship anyway, and learn it within the week.
4. After the merge, send the Phase 1 coach one line: what changed, what you wrote, what the AI wrote, one failure case. No IBM code.

The study plan stays as it is: it builds the fluency, office tasks give the practice.
