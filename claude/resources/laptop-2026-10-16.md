# Laptop fix, Oct 15-16 2026 (contingency, not study)

HP 15s-eq0xxx, Ryzen 5 3500U, Ubuntu 24.04 on external USB SSD. Owner of this record: Claude.

## Cause (proven from the journal, Oct 15)
- 8 GB stick showed 5.7 GiB usable: the iGPU reserves 2048 MB (`dmesg`: `VRAM: 2048M`).
- At 22:42 Oct 15: swap 4.0 of 4.0 GiB used, 1.6 GiB available.
- Last seconds of the frozen boot: `systemd-journald: Under memory pressure, flushing caches` x3, libinput "your system is too slow", 5 shutdown requests logged; no OOM kill ever fired. No suspend in that boot, so sleep was not the cause.

## Fix (Oct 16), verified
- Added Samsung 16 GB DDR4 SO-DIMM, `M471A2G43AB2-CWE` (part number read from the stick's SPD matches the label). Slot 1 Micron 8 GB channel A, slot 2 Samsung 16 GB channel B, both 2400 MT/s (CPU max). Cost Rs 12,700 fitted.
- `free -h`: 21 GiB total, swap 0 used after boot.
- `memtester 12G 1`: every test ok (58 min).
- Load test, 30 min: kind cluster + 5 nginx pods, Elasticsearch, Kafka, Prometheus, Grafana, second Postgres with pgbench at 300 tx/s, a 4 GB memory hog, plus Om's 3 VS Code, Cursor, DataGrip, YouTube, Spotify, pytest and go test runs. Lowest MemAvailable 5,409 MB; 139 MB swapped in over 30 min; no freeze. The script's FAIL line was a wrong bar (zero swap), fixed to "below 1 GB available".
- Cooling, Tctl over 2 min idle: lid open avg 57.5 (min 47.4); lid closed avg 62.7 (min 46.2); on a fan stand avg 47.1 (min 41.8, max 69.2). Cooling hardware healthy; use the stand.
- SSD on a 5 Gbps port with the `uas` driver (387 MB/s read). Internal NVMe (SK hynix BC511) reads 759 MB/s but keeps Windows and Om's files for now.

## Decisions (Om)
- No new laptop before the Gate A review.
- Windows stays on the NVMe until Om's files are backed up; later either reclaim "New Volume" for Ubuntu or drop Windows.

## Effect on the plan
- K1 kind and P1 Postgres work are no longer RAM-blocked.
- Hours go to contingency (s5: genuine surprise), Om to confirm the number at the Oct 18 close.
