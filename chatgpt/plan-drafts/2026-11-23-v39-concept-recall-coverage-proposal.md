# Proposal: concept-level spaced recall after v3.9 (2026-11-23)

**Status:** NEW RECOMMENDATION, NOT APPROVED. Section 9 change-control proposal, not an edit to the frozen master. **Trigger:** material execution defect: DRILLs are taught, run and then can vanish without a recall obligation. **Owner of decision:** Om. **Source:** plan/Phase1_Master_Plan_v3.9.md, sections 0, 5, 6.6, 6.12, 7, 9 and W7-W12 of section 14, plus the 2026-11-22 W7 close, both coach logs and outboxes.

## Decision proposed

**Approve four linked fixes, not a general redesign.** Keep NEW/COLD problem targets unchanged; keep existing D1/D3/D7/D14/D28/D90 re-solves for counted NEW problems; do not re-score past verdicts. Register every named teaching part, including a DSA DRILL or an independently taught bedside topic, with dated recall obligations. Use the funded five-minute warm-ups for non-DSA recall; explicitly budget additional DSA work. The checker audits the records but cannot claim Om remembers a concept from text alone.

### 1. BUG: DRILLs have no retention path

- **What:** Amend section 6.6 #5 and add a DRILL recall rule. A completed DRILL does not count as NEW and its reviews never count as the numbered COLD re-solves. It instead gets the label **DRILL-RECALL**: D7 = a five-minute cold trace, core invariant and one counterexample; D28 = a fifteen-minute blank-page implementation for the simpler drills (682, 374, insertion sort, factorial, Fibonacci), or twenty-five minutes for merge sort and quicksort, with a changed case; D90 = a five-minute cold transfer question or changed input. Om attempts before seeing notes, and the coach records HELD or SLIPPED with evidence. A SLIPPED result is scored once; teaching and any new retry are in later, separate DSA slots, never the same session (section 6.6 #11). An explicitly verified cold use of the *same mechanism* in another problem may satisfy the due check, but a general claim such as "trees use recursion" does not.
- **Why:** DRILL code can be correct on the teaching day with no evidence it can be recalled later. Section 6.6 #6 only schedules counted problem re-solves; section 6.12's warm-up excludes DSA.
- **Evidence:** Baseball 682 (Oct 3), Guess Number 374 (Oct 28), insertion sort (Nov 17-20, dsa-python PR #22), factorial (PR #23), Fibonacci (PR #24). Merge sort and quicksort remain in the W8 sorting line. The W7 close records the three November DRILLs as built, not retained. Stack and binary-search counted colds may give cross-credit only for a mechanism explicitly cold-checked and recorded. Tree problems 226/104 can give recursion credit only if the relevant call-stack/base-case mechanism is independently checked.
- **Time:** Five simpler DRILLs at 25 minutes each (5 + 15 + 5) and two harder sorting DRILLs at 35 minutes each (5 + 25 + 5) = **195 minutes (3h15m)**. Allocate **4 additional DSA hours** for these seven, leaving 45 minutes for setup/overrun, not assuming a beginner implements quicksort from scratch in ten minutes. Because 682 and 374 have missed early intervals, run **one current catch-up** rather than inventing historical checks; retain their future D90 check. Dates for merge and quicksort derive from the real completion date, never from the planned week. A new DRILL later adds its own honestly priced 20 minutes.
- **What moves:** No extra hours are placed on top of W8. The earliest recall items use the same week's DSA allocation and displace the equivalent amount of W8 new teaching (sorting/trees) into W9; that displaced work cascades forward at the Dec 6 rebuild. Dated counted COLDs on Nov 26, 27, 28 remain first. No Gate A date is asserted without a rebuilt map.
- **Risk reduced:** A correct one-off insertion sort is mistaken for a lasting skill; drill inflation of NEW/COLD totals; missed retention discovered only in an interview.

### 2. GAP: warm-ups remember the latest session, not the oldest due topic

- **What:** Amend section 6.12. For each named teaching part on **every** non-DSA track, record an independent recall obligation at D7, D28 and D90 from the actual date first proved. Keep the existing within-seven-days first proof. The five-minute desk warm-up takes the **oldest due tracked concept, from any non-DSA lane**; it revisits the last session only when no recall is due. One small blank-page question, trace or command with one changed input, no notes, HELD/SLIPPED with evidence. A real independent, dated test or code use satisfies a check only if the log names the precise mechanism tested. More than one item due stays visibly queued, not silently cleared. A miss is repaired per the non-DSA section 0 rule. Bedside cards remain optional bonus, never the required retention mechanism.
- **Why:** Returning to Go every week does not guarantee an old slice-aliasing concept is recalled. The track-level gap checker and last-session warm-up can both pass while older material is forgotten.
- **Evidence:** Section 6.12 explicitly watches tracks and the most recent session. The W7 close records six of six warm-ups but a 12-day Go restart and a previously taught L1 cohesion gap. The K2 ReplicaSets video of Nov 22 was teaching-only; a lab or first proof must precede any recall credit. The 875 bedside explanation has later counted DSA checks; sliding-window bedside teaching belongs to finding 3.
- **Time:** **0 incremental learner hours initially**, by replacing, not adding to, the existing funded 35.75-hour warm-up allowance. A five-minute block cannot honestly clear ten due concepts. Every weekly close reports queued and overdue items; sustained overflow triggers the section 5 measured-pace re-forecast, not invisible free bedside homework.
- **What moves:** The last-session warm-up moves behind older-due recall. No curriculum topic moves initially. If the warm-up queue repeatedly exceeds available blocks, add measured minutes at the Dec 6 review, displacing later work or moving gate dates, never dropping a proof.
- **Risk reduced:** A busy lane gives false confidence, and bedside-only teaching disappears because it has no mandatory review.

### 3. GAP: pre-plan DSA families have fallen out of the spaced system

- **What:** Adopt the bounded portion of Claude's 2026-11-22 draft. In the existing weekly six-question pattern drill, use **three previously solved pre-plan problems and three recent problems** per week in W8-W13 until all 16 pre-plan problems have appeared. This is a *replacement* within the six prompts, not six extra prompts. Require at least one later independent code re-solve from each of the four families: arrays/hashing, two pointers, sliding window and linked lists. Pick additional cold re-solves only when a family or problem's recognition/coding slips. The November 22 LC3 miss is already recorded; its bedside teaching took place in a later slot, so its new-wording recall must be after that lesson. Do not backdate earlier spaced checks or mark them PASS.
- **Why:** Recognition without code does not prove independent implementation, and counted recent problems do not cover all forgotten older families.
- **Evidence:** Claude's draft enumerates 16 problems: 217, 242, 1, 49, 347, 238, 128; 125, 167, 11, 344, 977; 121, 3, 643; 707. The Nov 22 pattern drill scored 4.5/6 and missed sliding window on Om's own LC3 (answer: "Dynamic Array"). The current W8-W12 rows have no dated re-solves for these families.
- **Time:** Six-question drill unchanged, so **no additional pattern-prompt slots**. Four representative code re-solves at about 15 minutes each = **1 hour minimum**; up to all 16 = **4 hours maximum** if the checks show widespread gaps. Teaching after misses is separately measured and cannot be assumed to fit the cap. No 1/6 observed miss is converted into a reliable forecast for 16 problems.
- **What moves:** Make recognition coverage happen in the existing weekly drill. Book the extra code checks after the fixed W8 COLD obligations and other higher-priority work, through the Dec 6 map rebuild. Their 1-4 hours move subsequent new DSA scope and possibly gate dates; nothing is charged to unmeasured phone time.
- **Risk reduced:** Arrays, two pointers, sliding window or linked-list recognition survives on paper while independent coding has gone rusty.

### 4. BUG in verification coverage: check_plan.py cannot detect an unreviewed concept

- **What:** Add a compact **Concept recall queue** to phase1-log.md, with stable topic ID, lane, teaching source/date, first proof/evidence, D7/D28/D90 due dates, each result/evidence or explicit same-mechanism credit, and next action. Any teaching-only bedside note gets a row but no fake PASS. The lesson-running coach records the row; the Sunday-close coach audits it using both coach records. Extend check_plan.py in two modes: (a) static plan validation asserts that DRILL and non-DSA recall rules and dated review capacity are present; (b) an optional audit of the shared recall queue flags a taught ID without a proof/review path, overdue unsatisfied items, future dates before teaching, and illegitimate NEW/COLD count changes. For a plan-level taught-ID inventory, a literal identifier is necessary; a text-only heuristic cannot enumerate every concept in free-form prose. Add negative tests for each omission and a passing control.
- **Why:** The current checker validates budgets, week caps, dependencies and the presence of certain text. It does not compare taught concepts to completed reviews.
- **Evidence:** Running the uploaded checker against the canonical 969-hour v3.9 file gave PASS, 87 weeks and 14 fragmentation warnings. Adding an extra named DRILL with no review to a scratch copy still gave PASS. This is a deliberately tested false negative, not proof of an implementation bug.
- **Time:** **0 learner hours** to build/commit the checker and queue format (coach work). Filling and updating records is coach administration; Om only performs the recall whose time is budgeted above. The script cannot verify human memory, so HELD/SLIPPED requires actual cold evidence.
- **What moves:** No study block. The coach who builds the accepted amendment runs the plan generator, the checker (clean PASS), negative tests (expected FAIL), and one reconciliation of the W8-W12 map. The other coach independently verifies. Nothing is promoted to plan/ until Om approves.
- **Risk reduced:** The plan passes mechanical checks while silently violating the every-concept retention rule.
